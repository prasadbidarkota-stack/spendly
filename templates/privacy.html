Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password, along with the expense records you create — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate your account, display your expense history, and generate the summaries and breakdowns shown in the app. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible through your authenticated session. You may request that your data be deleted by removing your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party services. Any future integrations that would involve sharing data externally will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
